## Decision

Foundation components remain unchanged and valid:

- JamVM L remains DEVICE-PASS.
- GNU Classpath archive remains immutable; do not patch `glibj.zip`.
- native startup/IPC is DEVICE-PASS.
- Golden RGB565 receive/publish/present is DEVICE-PASS for 240x320 real games.
- multi-game relaunch remains DEVICE-PASS.

The platform is **not yet globally STABLE** because the required resolution matrix is incomplete and KDTT still has an isolated PNG ICC-v4 compatibility failure.

The next admitted compatibility experiment must be source-level in the FreeJ2ME runtime (`PlatformImage` boundary), not a GNU Classpath byte patch. It may remove only the ancillary PNG `iCCP` chunk before `ImageIO.read`; PNG pixels/chunks and the immutable `glibj.zip` must otherwise remain untouched.
